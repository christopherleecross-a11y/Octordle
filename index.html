HOW TO PLAY

Guess 8 hidden 5-letter words at once. Every guess you make is applied to all 8 boards simultaneously.

KEYBOARD DOTS

Each letter key has 8 small dots — one per board. Each dot shows that letter's status on that board: green / yellow / gray / unused.

MODES

CLASSIC 13 guesses · CHILL 16 guesses · EXTREME 12 guesses.

Each difficulty also has a DAILY variant — the same 8 words for every player, refreshing once per day. One play per daily per day.
